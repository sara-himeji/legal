Piracy and copyright policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

1. Our position

Sara does not host, store, stream or provide video, manga or novel files. It posts news headlines with a short excerpt and a link to the original publisher, release alerts with links to AniList or MangaUpdates, and lookup results from AniList. It does not post links to unlicensed streams or downloads.

2. What is not allowed

  • Using the bot to share, request or link to pirated or otherwise infringing content.
  • Configuring feeds or roles to spread such links.

Server admins are responsible for what happens in the channels they configure.

3. How content appears

  • News comes from public feeds of the publishers. Article images are loaded from the publisher's own site, not copied.
  • /trace sends an image link to trace.moe, which only identifies the anime, episode and timestamp.
  • Some buttons link to legal retail pages, for example Amazon.

4. Related projects

The operator also publishes baka-cli, a separate open-source command-line tool. It is not part of the bot. The bot's /baka-cli command only shows a description and a link to its public repository. baka-cli can fetch from third-party websites that the operator does not control, and some of them may offer content without a licence. Using any tool to access content you have no right to is your own responsibility and may be unlawful where you live.

5. Reporting infringement

If you are a rights holder or their agent and think something posted by the bot infringes your rights, contact the operator via the support server and include:

  • the work you own and how to verify it;
  • where the material appears (server, channel, message link or URL);
  • your name and a way to reach you;
  • a statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised;
  • a statement that your notice is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorised to act for them.

Valid notices are reviewed promptly. Material is removed or disabled where justified, and the person affected may reply with a counter-notice.

6. Repeat infringers

Users or servers that repeatedly use the bot for infringing content can be blocked, and the bot can be removed from them.
